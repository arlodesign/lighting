How to play the lights

MiniLab 3 · 8 lights · already set up

Songs and looks

Songs
Turn the song knob (the one by the screen) to find the next song, click it to load. The song brings its own look, colors and tempo.
Keys
Each white key is a look, calm on the left, wild on the right. Play harder for brighter. Black keys do nothing.
Tap tempo
Tap pad 8 on the beat, 4 times or more. Everything follows the beat.

Pads (hold them)

Strobe
Press harder to flash faster.
White flash
Every light full white.
Blackout
Everything off (the panic button).
Color bump
All lights jump to the next color.
Left / Right
Flash one side of the stage white.
Lightning
Random lightning strikes.

Knobs

Speed
How fast the look moves, from ¼ to 8 × the beat.
Hue
Turns all the colors around the color wheel.
Color
How colorful: all the way down is white.
Palette
Picks a set of colors (Blood, Pumpkin, Sunset…).
Width
How much of the stage the look uses at once: a short or long chase tail, a thin or wide wave, few or many sparkles.
Fade
How slowly a new look or palette blends in: instant up to 4 beats.
Shape
Which way moves travel: Sweep left → right, Center out from the band, Split both sides together, Scatter hops around.
Variety
How different the colors are: one color, neighbors, three-way, opposites.

Faders and strips

Master
Overall brightness.
Energy
Down for the verse (dim, slow, one color), middle for the song as set, up for the chorus (bright, fast, more colors).
Balance
Middle = both sides. Up fades the right out, down the left.
Audio
Up makes the lights pulse with the band, and lets a loud chorus push Energy up on its own.
Spotlight
Slide a finger to move a white spot across the stage.
Swell
Push up to bring every light toward full brightness.

Lost? Bring Energy to the middle and load the song again with the song knob. Laptop backup: space tap, esc blackout, S strobe, F flash, 1–9 looks. Press ? on the laptop for the full list.
